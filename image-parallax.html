Image Parallax Architecture

A focused collection of 12 distinct, high-performance image parallax animations built strictly with GSAP 3 and ScrollTrigger. No animated typography distractions — pure image physics, spatial layering, mask clipping, and 3D optical depth.

Type 01 • Viewport Masked Drift

Classic Editorial Window Parallax

The image translates vertically inside a fixed-height clipping frame via normalized yPercent scrub. As you scroll past, the image moves at a dampened differential velocity to create a cinematic window look.

Type 02 • Multi-Plane Diorama

Multi-Layer 3D Spatial Depth Parallax

Four distinct depth planes (Sky 0.15x, Mountain Horizon 0.45x, Floating Subject 0.85x, and Ambient Foreground Lighting 1.35x) move at contrasting scroll velocities with drifting particle fields.

Type 03 • Scale & Panning Dynamics

Ken Burns Zoom & Focal Shift Parallax

Combines continuous camera scale compression (scale: 1.35 → 1.0) with diagonal focal point translation during scroll scrub, mimicking professional cinematic documentary zoom pans.

Type 04 • Polygon Clip-Path Geometry

Diagonal Curtain & Mask Reveal Parallax

The outer container expands via dynamic clip-path: polygon(...) angular cuts while the inner image drives in the inverse coordinate direction with scale deceleration.

Type 05 • Counter-Directional Flow

Opposing Dual-Column Asymmetric Stream

Side-by-side photographic columns scroll in reverse directions: Column A glides upward while Column B descends downward, creating a synchronized optical conveyor effect without jumpiness.

Type 06 • 3D Cursor Tracking & Glare

3D Interactive Gyroscope / Mouse Depth Parallax

Hover over the cards below. Smooth GSAP quickTo() transforms tilt the card in 3D perspective space (rotateX/Y) while the background image layer and floating graphics translate with realistic optical parallax and dynamic specular glare.

Type 07 • Pinned Track & Kinetic Skew

Pinned Horizontal Velocity Skew Parallax

The viewport pins while wide photographic cards glide horizontally across the screen. Inner images move at counter-velocities, and rapid scrolling induces dynamic kinetic skewing based on real-time ScrollTrigger velocity.

Type 08 • Focal Iris Magnification

Circular Iris & Geometric Lens Parallax

A high-contrast circular lens floats across an oversized muted architectural backdrop. As you scroll, the lens scales up, shifts coordinates with GPU acceleration, and exposes saturated focal details.

Type 09 • Fragment Assembly & Tear

Broken Vertical Slice Alignment Parallax

A single high-resolution image is divided into 5 vertical columns. The slices enter the viewport at heavily staggered, alternating vertical offsets, lock seamlessly into a unified master image at screen center, and disperse apart upon scrolling past.

Type 10 • Spatial Deck Peeling

Stacked 3D Depth Peeling Cards Parallax

The viewport pins while cards in a 3D perspective stack peel away into the negative z-space with realistic rotation (rotateX: 18deg) and opacity dissipation, revealing underlying visual layers.

Type 11 • Elastic Cursor Gravitation

Elastic Magnetic Proximity Parallax

Move your cursor across each photo tile. The card body is pulled gravitationally towards the pointer with spring dampening while the internal image moves in reverse offset to create a tactile 3D lens refraction effect.

Type 12 • Double Exposure Screen Blend

Dual-Layer Blended Silhouette Parallax

A double-exposure visual composite where a foreground geometric silhouette is composited using CSS mix-blend-mode: screen over a moving high-contrast seascape texture, scrolling with opposite vertical momentum.
